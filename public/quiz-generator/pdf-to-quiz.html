Creating comprehensive assessments has never been easier. Simply upload your lecture notes, textbook chapters, or study materials, and our AI analyzes the content to generate questions that test true understanding rather than simple memorization. The process takes just minutes and produces questions that educators can use immediately or customize to fit their specific needs.

Our AI-powered system understands the nuances of subject content, identifying key concepts, important relationships, and critical thinking opportunities. Whether you're teaching introductory concepts or advanced topics, QuizForge adapts to generate appropriate questions for your level.

  1. Upload Content: Add your materials in PDF, DOCX, or text format. You can upload multiple documents at once for comprehensive coverage. Our system extracts text from PDFs and Word documents automatically, preserving formatting and structure.
  2. Configure Settings: Choose question types (multiple choice, true/false, short answer, essay) and difficulty level. Set the number of questions you need and specify any particular topics or themes you want emphasized in the quiz.
  3. Generate Quiz: AI creates subject-specific questions in under 2 minutes, analyzing your content for key concepts and important details. Each question includes an explanation and is tagged with difficulty level and learning objective.

The generated questions follow pedagogical best practices, testing different levels of understanding from basic recall to complex analysis and synthesis. This ensures your assessments measure true comprehension and help identify areas where students need additional support.
